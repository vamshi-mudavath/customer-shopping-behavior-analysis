Key findings (all 3,900 orders)

From the Python and SQL analysis in the notebook

  1. Clothing and Accessories bring in 76.5% of the USD 233,081 revenue; Outerwear is only 7.9%.
  2. Order size is flat everywhere: the average order stays around USD 58–62 across gender, age group, season, shipping, subscription and discount.
  3. Subscriptions are not lifting spend: subscribers average USD 59.49 per order vs 59.87 for non-subscribers. 72% of repeat buyers still have not subscribed.
  4. 43% of orders use a discount, with no gain in basket size. Hats, Sneakers and Coats are discounted almost half the time.
  5. The base is mature: 80% of customers are Loyal (more than 10 previous purchases); only 83 customers (2%) are new.
  6. Fall is the strongest season (USD 60,018, highest average order of USD 61.56); Summer is the weakest (USD 55,777).
Data caveat: discounts and subscriptions are recorded only for male customers in this dataset, so gender comparisons on those two factors are not reliable.
